About Us

A trusted academic institute, built around the whole student.

System Academic Care was founded to close the gap between classroom teaching and real exam readiness. Under the leadership of Sumon Khan, we bring together experienced educators, structured video learning, and carefully written books into a single, dependable system for students from Class IV to XII.

  • I.
    Academic Coaching — Regular classroom sessions across all subjects and sections, following the Bangla version curriculum.
  • II.
    Video-Based Learning — HD recorded lectures with lifetime access, so revision never depends on a fixed schedule.
  • III.
    Printed Books — Academic books, notes, and practice sheets prepared by our own teaching team.
  • IV.
    Exam Preparation Support — Model tests, doubt-solving, and progress tracking through every academic year.
